Percentage Formula

To calculate X% of Y, use:

(X × Y) ÷ 100

Example: 20% of 500 = (20 × 500) ÷ 100 = 100.

Percentage Calculator FAQ

What is a percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. For example, 25% means 25 out of 100.

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the percentage by the number and divide the result by 100. For example, 20% of 500 is 100.
