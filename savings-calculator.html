FAQ

Savings Calculator Questions

What is a savings calculator?

A savings calculator estimates how your savings may grow based on an initial amount, regular contributions, an estimated interest rate and the amount of time you save.

Does this calculator include monthly contributions?

Yes. The calculator includes your monthly contribution and assumes contributions are added at the end of each month.

How is interest calculated?

This calculator uses monthly compounding based on the annual interest rate you enter.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. The result is an estimate for informational purposes. Actual savings growth can differ because rates, fees, taxes, contribution timing and account conditions may vary.

Is the Savings Calculator free?

Yes. KROC LABS provides this calculator as a free online tool. No signup is required.
