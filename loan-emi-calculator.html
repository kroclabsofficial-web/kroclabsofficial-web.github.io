EMI Formula

The standard reducing-balance EMI formula is:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

P is the principal loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Loan EMI Calculator FAQ

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the regular payment made toward repaying a loan, including principal and interest.

What information do I need to calculate EMI?

You need the loan amount, annual interest rate and loan term. This calculator uses those values to estimate the monthly payment.

Does a higher interest rate increase EMI?

Generally, for the same loan amount and term, a higher interest rate results in a higher estimated EMI and higher total interest.

Is this Loan EMI Calculator free?

Yes. The KROC LABS Loan EMI Calculator is designed as a free online tool and does not require an account.

Is the calculated EMI guaranteed by a lender?

No. The result is an estimate based on the information entered. Actual loan payments can vary depending on the lender, fees, taxes, rate structure and loan agreement.

Can I use this calculator on my phone?

Yes. The page is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and desktop browsers.
