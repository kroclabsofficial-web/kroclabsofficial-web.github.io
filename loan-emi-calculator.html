EMI Formula

The calculator uses the standard EMI formula for loans with a fixed monthly interest rate.

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

P = Loan Principal, r = Monthly Interest Rate, n = Total Number of Monthly Payments
