Compound Interest Formula

The standard compound interest formula used by this calculator is:

A = P × (1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ

P is the starting amount, r is the annual interest rate expressed as a decimal, n is the number of compounding periods per year, and t is the time in years.

Compound Interest Example

If you start with 10,000, earn an annual rate of 8% and compound the interest monthly for 5 years, the calculator estimates the final amount using monthly compounding.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Compound Interest Calculator FAQ

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on an initial amount and on the interest accumulated during previous periods.

What information do I need?

You need the starting amount, annual interest rate, investment time and compounding frequency.

What does compounding frequency mean?

Compounding frequency describes how often interest is added to the balance. Common frequencies include annually, quarterly, monthly and daily.

Does more frequent compounding always produce a higher result?

For the same positive nominal annual rate and time period, more frequent compounding generally produces a higher mathematical result. Actual investment products may use different terms and conditions.

Is this calculator free?

Yes. The KROC LABS Compound Interest Calculator is designed as a free online tool and does not require an account.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. The calculator provides a mathematical estimate based on the values entered. Actual investment returns, rates, fees, taxes and product terms can differ.
