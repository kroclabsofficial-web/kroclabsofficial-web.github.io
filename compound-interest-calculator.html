Compound Interest Formula

The standard compound growth formula is:

A = P × (1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ

A is the final amount, P is the starting amount, r is the annual interest rate as a decimal, n is the number of compounding periods per year, and t is the time in years.

Simple Example

If you start with 10,000 at an annual rate of 8%, compounded annually for 5 years, the calculator estimates the final amount using the compound growth formula.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Compound Interest Calculator FAQ

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the starting amount plus previously accumulated interest.

What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest is generally calculated only on the original principal, while compound interest also accounts for previously accumulated interest.

What does compound frequency mean?

Compound frequency describes how often interest is added to the balance. Common frequencies include annually, half-yearly, quarterly, monthly and daily.

Does this calculator include monthly deposits?

No. This calculator estimates compound growth on a starting amount only. Regular contributions are not included in this calculation.

Is the result guaranteed?

No. The result is a mathematical estimate based on the values entered. Actual investment returns or interest rates may vary.
